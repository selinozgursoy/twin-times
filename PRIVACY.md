# Privacy

Twin News is local-first.

- Stories, preferences, Newsmarks, and Information Diet events are stored in Chrome extension local storage.
- Twin News does not include analytics, advertising, accounts, or a remote Twin backend.
- Twin News does not request the `history` permission.
- Network requests are used to retrieve configured news feeds and to open article links you choose.
- Removing the extension or clearing its site/extension data can remove locally stored Twin data.
